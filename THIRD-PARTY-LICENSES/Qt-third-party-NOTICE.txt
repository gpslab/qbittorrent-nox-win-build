Third-party code bundled inside the Qt 6 libraries
==================================================

The Qt 6 DLLs shipped in this bundle (in particular Qt6Core.dll) statically
incorporate third-party components under their own permissive licenses. The most
significant one is PCRE2, which Qt uses for regular-expression support and which
is compiled into Qt6Core. Its license and copyright are reproduced below.
